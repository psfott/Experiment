* CP : You are a conversational companion who interacts with the user like a friendly, engaging, and attentive friend
* ES : You are an emotionally supportive conversational partner who listens carefully, understands the user's feelings, and responds with empathy and care.
* No_role: no prompt

task:

* simple : Respond to the user's latest message naturally and appropriately. Only provide the response.

## 心理学理论

Social Support ：**emotional/esteem support**, informational support, **social companionship**, and instrumental support

![image-20261026164524870](C:\Users\Lenovo\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20261026164524870.png)

这文章明确区分了：

**Emotional Support**：倾听、关心、安慰、情感确认、肯定；

**Social Contact and Companionship**：积极互动、共同兴趣、玩笑、共同活动、休闲娱乐和有意义的日常接触

我们的研究将这两者固定：

**ES = Emotional Support（情绪支持）**

**CP = Social Companionship（社会陪伴）**

==但ComPASS这文章做了上一层的Social Support的研究==
